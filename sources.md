# Suggested documents (all public)

Download the PDFs from the issuing agency's site (search the title) and save
them in `docs/` with descriptive file names.

Model risk and AI governance
- SR 11-7 / OCC Bulletin 2011-12: Supervisory Guidance on Model Risk Management
- OCC Comptroller's Handbook: Model Risk Management (2021)
- NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0)
- NIST AI 600-1: Generative AI Profile
- The newer guidance you cite in your articles (SR 26-2, OCC Bulletin 2026-13)

Third parties and consumer protection
- Interagency Guidance on Third-Party Relationships: Risk Management (2023)
- CFPB Circular 2022-03 and 2023-03 (adverse action notices and complex algorithms)

Optional, for a different flavor
- Risk Factors sections of a few bank 10-K filings (SEC EDGAR)

Mixing regulator guidance with your own Substack articles makes a good demo:
"ask my writing and the rules it references."
